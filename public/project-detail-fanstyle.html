FANSTYLE

About the project

FANSTYLE is an Okinawa developer of new-build and renovated condominiums. Its website has to sell a lifestyle as much as a home, and turn interest into model-room visits and consultations.

The home page opens on a full-bleed slider of its flagship properties, such as YOMITAN Premium Residence, with a direct line to the sales team. It then lays out the latest condominiums, current campaigns, news, the company’s own radio show, Facebook and Instagram feeds, a YouTube channel and two closing calls to action for investors and landowners.

A white canvas, large serif display type and the brand’s single signal red keep the page calm and editorial, so the architecture photography leads and every enquiry button stands out.
