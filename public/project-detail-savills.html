SAVILLS VIETNAM

About the project

Savills Vietnam manages and leases office buildings across the country. This project is the team’s internal platform: one place to follow every building, unit, contact and deal, instead of spreadsheets passed between departments.

The dashboard opens on the month’s active opportunities, new deals, billing and expected revenue, above the full property list. From there the team drills into a building’s details, photos and drawings, checks unit availability floor by floor, manages the contact database and moves each deal from first contact to close, then builds weekly revenue reports with their own charts.

The interface keeps to white working surfaces with Savills’ yellow and red used only for status, highlights and charts, so dense tables stay easy to scan.
