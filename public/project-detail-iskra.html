ISKRA

About the project

ISKRA is a Web3 gaming platform where players earn while they play. Its Pioneer NFTs give holders a share of the platform: node operation rewards, community rewards, governance staking and airdrops for upcoming games.

The project covers the platform’s main journeys: discovering the games on the home page, buying and staking Pioneer NFTs step by step, tracking Contribution Points through the ISKRA Community Tier, trading on the DEX and managing tokens in the wallet, plus sign-up, sign-in and password reset.

The interface sits on a deep navy night sky, with an isometric illustration world and a blue-to-pink gradient taken from the ISKRA mark, so complex crypto steps feel like part of a game.
