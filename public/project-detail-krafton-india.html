KRAFTON INDIA

About the project

KRAFTON India is the Indian arm of KRAFTON, the company behind PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS. This project is its corporate website, built around one line: “Connecting India through play”.

The site moves section by section through full-screen slides that introduce the company and its standing in India’s mobile gaming market, its mission, its games, esports, news and careers. Each slide leads with one bold statement and lets real figures, game art and photography do the rest.

The visual language keeps to black and white with a condensed, all-caps display face, so the colour comes from the games and the people. A language switcher serves IN, KO, CN, JP and EN.
