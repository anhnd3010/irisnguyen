NAVER CONNECT

About the project

소프트웨어야 놀자 (“Software, let’s play”) is NAVER Connect Foundation’s coding and AI education programme for children. This project designed its AI & Data practice: browser-based lessons where students from the third grade up work alongside a friendly robot, Sonolbot, to run a smart farm.

Each lesson starts from a short programme page, then moves into an illustrated practice space. In Grow a Fruit Farm and AI Smart Farm, students label fruit images, train a classification model and watch the robot sort and harvest the crop, so ideas such as supervised learning, image data and model bias are learned by doing.

The visual language pairs a bright violet with mint and pink accents, soft ribbon shapes and a cast of robot characters, keeping abstract ideas playful and approachable for young learners.
