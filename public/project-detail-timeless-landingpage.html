TIMELESS LANDINGPAGE

About the project

TIMELESS buys pre-owned luxury goods — watches, jewellery, bags, precious metals and more — with appraisal modelled on Japanese department stores. This landing page has one job: earn enough trust in a single scroll that visitors start a KakaoTalk consultation.

The page leads with the promise and a consultation button, then backs it up with key figures, the categories TIMELESS buys, reassurance for worn or broken items, three ways to sell (in store, at home or by mail), five promises, a short case for the service, an FAQ and contact details. Four hero variants — pocket watch, hourglass, wristwatch and chess king — explore the opening image.

Black, white and a single bright yellow keep the page quiet and premium, letting the photography carry the sense of craft while the yellow marks every call to action.
