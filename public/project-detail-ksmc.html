Keystone Marketing Company

About the project

Keystone Marketing Company is a full-service digital design and marketing agency in Thailand, working with brands such as Samsung, DHL Express, Naver Webtoon and PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS. This project is its company website, made in collaboration with HIVELAB.

The site leads with the work. A grid of campaigns opens into case pages, from Galaxy S24 Unpacked and DHL’s lead generation campaign to a Line Webtoon pop-up store, short-form video for Kitsch Catch and PUBG’s global social channels. News, an About page with the agency’s services, its Thailand team and regional offices, and a project contact form complete the site.

The pages stay black and white, with an iridescent chrome accent and a single yellow highlight, so each client’s campaign brings the colour. Everything is set in Cairo and switches between Thai and English.
