User flow A: find an adviser, consult and come back
From Home, users check the Ranking, search by topic in the Supporter list, or get a notice that a favourite adviser is online, then reach Supporter detail. Without enough points they buy or earn some and come back. With enough points they choose chat to open Chat detail, or a call: confirm the call, connecting, and if the adviser doesn’t answer the app suggests switching to chat. When the adviser answers: on the call, a quick top-up to keep talking, end the call and see the summary. Both branches end at Review (Good/Bad, can be skipped) and the consultation is saved in Chat history.

User flow B: sign up through the conversational karte
On first opening the app, users see the First screen. With an account, they sign in with email and password; if they forget the password they go through Account reissue 1 (enter email) and Account reissue 2 (new password) and sign in again, and a wrong entry shows one of 5 error cases. Without an account, the conversational karte asks in turn for a display name, gender, date of birth and concerns (up to 5). An invalid name or date of birth gets an error right in the chat bubble so it can be re-entered or re-picked; under 18, sign-up stops. Finally users review the karte card, edit any item and enter Home.

User flow: trying the API
From the homepage, a developer reaches Docs: Getting Your API Key through the Developer Hub or Explore API docs, or goes straight to the dashboard through Start Building Free. Without an API key, they sign up and create one on the dashboard, then return to Docs: Quick Example, copy the sample code and call the API. A 200 response means the call succeeded; otherwise they check Docs: Error Handling, fix the request and try again.

User flow: requesting an enterprise quote
From the homepage, the buyer opens Pricing to compare plans and read the FAQ, or goes straight to Contact Sales. If they need the Enterprise plan, they open Contact Sales, fill in the form and submit it; if not, Get Started takes them to sign up for the Basic plan on the dashboard. When every required field is filled in and the email is valid, Sales follows up; otherwise errors appear under each field so the form can be fixed and resubmitted.
